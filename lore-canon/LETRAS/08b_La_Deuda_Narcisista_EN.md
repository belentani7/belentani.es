*Written 2026-09-29 from the author's own brief. Equivalent to `08_..._ES.md`.*

- **Tempo:** 74 BPM (half-time 148) · **4/4** · **Key:** F# minor
- **Context:** same rhythm bed as *La Deuda*. Dutch/one narrator until the author enters and takes the film.

# PART I — CODE RED *(the narcissist speaks)*

I've got a victim who walked off the page. (9)

## [Bridge 1 — hypocrisy]
